About Amul

(AMUL) Anand milk union limited dairy cooperative based in Anand Gujarat, India, has been a model of success since 1948. This cooperative has been studied by experts from various fields, including planning, economics, administration, and journalism.

The cooperative's business model, known as the 'Anand Pattern', empowers small producers by integrating their efforts to achieve economies of scale.

This approach revolutionized India's dairy cooperative movement, improving the lives of millions of dairy farmers and making India the world's largest milk producer.

The cooperative has grown humbly, prioritizing the growth and well-being of its farmers. Today, it is India's largest food brand, with products available nationwide.
